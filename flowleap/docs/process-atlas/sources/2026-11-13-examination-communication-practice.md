# Examination communication (Art. 94(3) EPC) in a small German patent firm: practice facts

Research date: 2026-11-13. This file is not legal advice. A patent attorney must review it. It describes how a firm of 2 to 15 attorneys handles an Art. 94(3) EPC communication today. It does not decide the FlowLeap design.

Labels:

- SOURCED: I read the page (or the full decision text), and it says this.
- SOURCED (secondary): I saw only a summary, a search extract, or a Case Law Book summary. I did not read the primary text.
- REPO: the fact is already in a repo source file; I did not read the page again. The repo fact keeps its own label.
- INFERRED: my conclusion. The fact says from what.

Quotes are at most 15 words. "Rn." = Randnummer in the official BGH text. Repo files: `2026-11-10-de-fristenkontrolle.md` (DE), `2026-11-10-epc-due-care.md` (EPC), `2026-11-10-epo-myepo-sources.md` (MyEPO), `../../2026-11-03-augmented-firm-research.md` section 2 (AFR).

Method note: EPO Guidelines pages carry about 100 000 characters of navigation; I read the text after that offset with a fetch tool that summarises. I read BGH XII ZB 31/23 as full text with `pdftotext`. I sent no request to an EPO system other than public epo.org pages.

## 1. Arrival, intake, docketing, date check

1. The EPO sends the communication to the firm's company Mailbox in MyEPO, not to one person. The company administrator chooses which users can handle Mailbox communications. REPO (MyEPO facts 17, 19; SOURCED there). https://link.epo.org/web/myepo-services/interact/en-myepo-feature-guide-handling-mailbox-communications.pdf
2. With an activated Mailbox, the EPO sends no paper copy in parallel (OJ EPO 2026, A20, Art. 2(5)). So post is not the normal channel for a firm on MyEPO. REPO (MyEPO fact 2; EPC fact 46; SOURCED there). https://www.epo.org/en/legal/official-journal/2026/03/a20
3. "Handled" status in the Mailbox is company-wide. The Mailbox has folders but no assignment to a person and no comment. REPO (MyEPO facts 20, 22; AFR 2.5). Same URL as fact 1.
4. Email alerts are a courtesy only; a user "may not invoke the omission of an alert" (OJ EPO 2023, A89, Art. 6(3)). REPO (MyEPO fact 8; SOURCED there). https://www.epo.org/en/legal/official-journal/2023/10/a89.html
5. The MyEPO Actions screen lists Art. 94(3) replies with their deadlines. It is not a complete docket. REPO (MyEPO facts 29, 30; SOURCED there).
6. Training content of the Patentanwaltsfachangestellte (PAFA): process incoming and outgoing post (ReNoPat-AusbV, Anlage, Abschnitt A, Nr. 2.2 a); "Fristen nach Eingang ... berechnen, notieren" and control them, and keep a Termin- und Fristenkalender (Abschnitt A, Nr. 2.3); compute deadlines, monitor expiry, check the conditions for Weiterbehandlung and Wiedereinsetzung (Abschnitt E, Nr. 4.1). Deadline computation is an exam subject (§ 10). SOURCED. https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/renopatausbv_2015/BJNR149000014.html
7. So, in a small firm, the PAFA opens the Mailbox item, matches it to the file, and enters the deadline and a Vorfrist in the docket (for example PATORG). INFERRED from fact 6 and AFR 2.1 and 2.4 (AFR marks the workflow as inferred too).
8. The lawyer may delegate computation and entry of routine deadlines to a well-trained, tested and supervised clerk, with clear instructions and "zumindest stichprobenartige Kontrolle". The BGH applies this to patent attorneys (X ZB 17/12, Rn. 10). REPO (DE facts 5, 11, 12; SOURCED there).
9. Staff must not change or delete a calendar deadline on their own; the attorney must forbid this by general instruction (X ZB 17/12, Leitsatz a). REPO (DE fact 8; SOURCED there).
10. An electronic calendar needs a control of each entry: a printout of the entered items or an error log (III ZB 96/18; XI ZB 23/08). Two employees who enter data under the "Vier-Augen-Prinzip" do not replace this control (III ZB 82/23, Rn. 10). REPO (DE facts 18, 22, 30; SOURCED there).
11. No BGH decision read requires a second person to check each entry. REPO (DE "What the sources do not say"). The "Vier-Augen-Prinzip" is firm practice, not a BGH rule. INFERRED from that.
12. EPO standard: the system must "normally work well" with "an independent, effective cross-check"; the Guidelines say this does not apply to relatively small entities (E-VIII 3.2). But T 428/98 required a cross-check in a six-person office. REPO (EPC facts 8, 28 to 30).
13. When the file is presented to the attorney for the deadline-bound act, the attorney must check the deadline personally (IX ZB 17/18, Rn. 10; XII ZB 483/21). For patent attorneys this duty may be narrower because of the number of deadlines (X ZB 17/12, Rn. 14). EPO side: once the file is presented, responsibility "passes over to him in all respects" (T 439/06). REPO (DE facts 7, 12, 13; EPC fact 40).
14. The BGH requires a Vorfrist in addition to the main deadline for grounds-of-appeal periods (XII ZB 31/23, Rn. 14: "durch eine ausreichende Vorfrist sicherzustellen"). REPO (DE fact 11; SOURCED there).
15. XII ZB 31/23, Rn. 22: in that case the Vorfrist ran "spätestens eine Woche vor Ablauf" of the noted grounds deadline. Rn. 23: the Vorfrist presentation exists so that time for checking and work remains if something goes wrong. SOURCED. https://www.bundesgerichtshof.de/SharedDocs/Entscheidungen/DE/Zivilsenate/XII_ZS/2023/XII_ZB__31-23.pdf?__blob=publicationFile&v=1
16. No source read sets a Vorfrist length for an EPO four-month period. The one-week figure in fact 15 is a ZPO appeal case. INFERRED (absence in sources read).

## 2. Reading and the reply strategy

17. The attorney reads the communication and decides the strategy. The epi Code makes a Member "responsible for the acts of assistants who are not Members" (Art. 3(e)) and forbids office work by a non-Member "without adequate supervision" (Art. 3(d)). SOURCED. https://www.epo.org/en/legal/official-journal/2025/06/a42
18. A representative cannot hand off tasks that fall to him personally, for example interpreting laws and treaties. Preparing a notice of appeal is not routine (T 2450/16). REPO (EPC facts 37, 39). The substantive analysis of an Art. 94(3) communication is therefore attorney work. INFERRED from these facts.
19. A Patentanwaltskandidat trains at least 34 months, 26 of them with a patent attorney (§ 7 PAO), and drafts and analyses under review. REPO (AFR 2.1, cited there). That a candidate or junior prepares the first analysis is INFERRED; no source read describes this step.
20. The PAFA training covers informing the client about the procedure and the status ("Verfahrensstand ... feststellen und dem Auftraggeber mitteilen", Abschnitt E, Nr. 2.3 a, 3.4 a), not the substantive analysis. SOURCED. https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/renopatausbv_2015/BJNR149000014.html
21. What counts as a reply under Art. 94(4): comment on at least one objection, or file amendments. A request for extension or for a consultation is not a reply. A request for a decision on the state of the file is a reply. SOURCED (Guidelines 2026 C-IV 3). https://www.epo.org/en/legal/guidelines-epc/2026/c_iv_3.html
22. The examiner repeats communications "as often as necessary" and aims at a final position in few actions; if objections remain, refusal may follow after at least one communication (Art. 113(1)). SOURCED. Same URL.

## 3. Report to the client and the instruction

23. epi Code Art. 4(a): "A Member must keep Clients informed of the status of their cases." The Code has no specific rule on reporting office communications. SOURCED. https://www.epo.org/en/legal/official-journal/2025/06/a42
24. epi Code Art. 1(g): a breach of the Code "cannot be justified by referring to instructions from a Client". SOURCED. Same URL.
25. T 112/89: telling the client about a deadline does not fully discharge the representative's own duty; without needed instructions, the representative must take all necessary steps to get them. T 1401/05: where communication with the client worked efficiently, the representative could rely on the client's awareness; no further reminder was required. SOURCED (secondary, Case Law Book summary). https://www.epo.org/en/legal/case-law/2025/clr_iii_e_5_5_2_a.html
26. J 15/14: between two representatives' offices, the instructed office must confirm receipt and execution; if no confirmation comes, the instructing office should follow up. SOURCED (secondary, Case Law Book summary). Same URL.
27. A European patent attorney's blog describes the reporting letter on an examination report: agency clients (foreign attorneys who instruct the EP firm) and direct clients need different letters; "for agency clients the reporting letter is often the only opportunity" to give input; direct clients want proposals to approve; the client may instruct abandonment. SOURCED (practitioner blog, 2013, UK-based). https://hollyip.blogspot.com/2013/10/advice-to-european-patent-attorneys-on.html
28. After the ten-day rule ended, a UK firm says attorneys report deadlines under the new rules and clients must not add ten days; clients should send instructions well before the deadline. SOURCED (firm page). https://www.potterclarkson.com/news/frequently-asked-questions-about-the-end-of-the-ten-day-rule
29. PAFA training includes correspondence "in englischer Sprache" with clients, correspondent attorneys and offices (Abschnitt E, Nr. 3.1 b). SOURCED. https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/renopatausbv_2015/BJNR149000014.html
30. Workflow in AFR 2.4: the attorney reads and writes the report; the client instructs by email, often near the end of the period; for a foreign client the firm reports to the associate, and two firms docket the same deadline. REPO (AFR 2.4; marked inferred there, except the vendor claim).
31. So the usual sequence is: report letter with the communication attached, the attorney's analysis and recommendation, and a request for instructions by a firm-set date before the EPO deadline. INFERRED from facts 23 to 30. No German source read states this sequence or a typical number of days.

## 4. The period

32. Art. 94(3): the Examining Division invites observations "as often as necessary"; Art. 94(4): no reply in time means the application is deemed withdrawn. REPO (EPC fact 52; SOURCED there).
33. Rule 132: periods set by the EPO are two to four months, six in special cases. REPO (EPC fact 53; SOURCED there).
34. Guidelines 2026 E-VIII 1.2: communications of an examining or opposition division "on substantive matters – four months". Objections under Art. 52, 53, 54, 56, 82, 83 are normally substantive. SOURCED. https://www.epo.org/en/legal/guidelines-epc/2026/e_viii_1_2.html
35. Guidelines 2026 E-VIII 1.6.1 (Rule 132 extension): request in writing "before expiry of the period that has been set"; a request "even if filed without reasons" is normally allowed for up to two months if the total period is not over six months. Longer only in exceptional cases, for example serious illness; leave or pressure of other work is not accepted. The extended period runs from the start of the original period. A request removes the application from PACE. SOURCED. https://www.epo.org/en/legal/guidelines-epc/2026/e_viii_1_6_1.html
36. E-VIII 1.6.1: if a timely request is refused, the loss of rights can be overcome only by a Rule 112(2) decision and/or further processing. SOURCED. Same URL.
37. Further processing (Art. 121, Rule 135(1)): pay the fee within two months of the communication of the loss of rights, and complete the omitted act. REPO (EPC facts 59, 60; SOURCED there). Re-establishment (Art. 122, Rule 136) is excluded where further processing is available. REPO (EPC fact 5).
38. Notification: since 1 November 2023 the document is deemed delivered on the date it bears; the ten-day rule is abandoned; seven-day safeguard. For the Mailbox, the document reaches its destination on the date it is made available; opening it is not a condition. REPO (EPC facts 42, 44 to 46; MyEPO facts 2, 10, 11; SOURCED there). Note: the brief said "Rule 126(2) new"; for the Mailbox the rule is Rule 127(2); Rule 126(2) is postal. REPO (MyEPO facts 12, 13).
39. From 1 April 2027 Rules 126 to 128 are deleted; new Rule 125(2) keeps the date-it-bears rule for documents dated from then. REPO (MyEPO fact 15; SOURCED there).
40. A firm says the end of the ten-day rule shortens the time for instructions and makes accurate docketing more important, because "few further remedies are available". SOURCED (firm page). https://www.potterclarkson.com/news/frequently-asked-questions-about-the-end-of-the-ten-day-rule

## 5. Night, holiday, illness: the deputy

41. § 46 PAO "Bestellung einer Vertretung", Abs. 1: "Der Patentanwalt muss für seine Vertretung sorgen" if he cannot practise for more than two weeks or will be away from the office for more than two weeks. Abs. 2: the deputy should be another patent attorney or a Rechtsanwalt (others possible, see Abs. 2). Abs. 3, 4: the Patentanwaltskammer appoints a deputy on application, or on its own if the attorney fails to act. SOURCED. https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/patanwo/__46.html
42. § 48 PAO is the Abwickler (person who winds up the practice) after death or lapse of admission, appointed by the Kammer, normally for one year. It is not for absence. SOURCED. https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/patanwo/__48.html
43. BGH XII ZB 31/23, Rn. 20: a lawyer must make general arrangements so that a deputy does the necessary acts if he is ill; concrete measures only if he can foresee the absence. Rn. 21 to 22: the firm did not show its deputy arrangements; the file was not presented to a deputy at the Vorfrist; this was a cause of the missed deadline. The case cites § 53 BRAO (the Rechtsanwalt rule) for an absence of over one week. SOURCED. https://www.bundesgerichtshof.de/SharedDocs/Entscheidungen/DE/Zivilsenate/XII_ZS/2023/XII_ZB__31-23.pdf?__blob=publicationFile&v=1
44. epi Code Art. 1(d): a Member who is prevented from practising "must take measures to safeguard the interests of the Member's Clients". SOURCED. https://www.epo.org/en/legal/official-journal/2025/06/a42
45. EPO case law on absences: J 41/92, a one-person office needs provisions for illness, for example cooperation with colleagues; T 387/11, a sole representative met due care by arranging cover by another representative; T 324/90, a large firm needs "an effective system of staff substitution"; T 122/91, no due care when the head of office left without telling his deputy about a time-critical matter; T 1401/05, no substitute needed for a two-day illness in a firm with few applications. SOURCED (secondary, Case Law Book summary). https://www.epo.org/en/legal/case-law/2025/clr_iii_e_5_4_5.html
46. Night: a Mailbox document is notified on the date it is made available, not when someone reads it (fact 38). So a document that arrives at night or during a holiday starts the period at once. INFERRED from fact 38. T 1289/10 asks for an email check at least at the end of each business day where the firm gave an email address for urgent mail. SOURCED (secondary). https://www.epo.org/en/legal/case-law/2025/clr_iii_e_5_5_2_a.html
47. Firm practice for a deputy in a 2 to 15 attorney firm (a named colleague for each attorney, presentation of Vorfrist files to that colleague) is INFERRED from facts 41, 43 and 45. No firm source read describes it.
