# German standard for deadline control (Fristenkontrolle) — primary-source facts

Research date: 2026-11-10. This file is not legal advice. It does not say whether a software-only confirmation of deadlines is permitted.

Method: statutes read on gesetze-im-internet.de. BGH decisions downloaded as PDF from the official BGH decision search (bundesgerichtshof.de, "Entscheidungssuche") and read in full text with pdftotext. Labels:

- SOURCED: I read the primary page or full decision text, and it says this.
- SOURCED (secondary): only a search extract or a secondary summary.
- NOT VERIFIED: I could not read a primary source.

Quotes are at most 15 words. "Rn." = Randnummer in the official BGH text.

## 1. Statutes

1. § 123 Abs. 1 PatG: a person who was prevented "ohne Verschulden" from keeping a deadline toward the DPMA or the BPatG is reinstated on application. Exceptions: opposition period and fee, opponent's appeal against maintenance, and priority-claiming filings (§ 7 Abs. 2, § 40). SOURCED. https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/patg/__123.html
2. § 123 Abs. 2 PatG: application in writing "innerhalb von zwei Monaten nach Wegfall des Hindernisses"; facts must be stated and made credible (glaubhaft); the omitted act must be done within the application period; absolute bar one year after the missed deadline expired. SOURCED. https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/patg/__123.html
3. § 233 ZPO: reinstatement if a party was prevented without fault from keeping a Notfrist or a period to state grounds of appeal, revision, etc.; absence of fault is presumed if the notice of legal remedy was missing or wrong ("Ein Fehlen des Verschuldens wird vermutet"). SOURCED. https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/zpo/__233.html
4. § 85 Abs. 2 ZPO: "Das Verschulden des Bevollmächtigten steht dem Verschulden der Partei gleich." BVerfG (2 BvL 26/81, 20.04.1982) held Abs. 2 compatible with the Basic Law (footnote on the page). SOURCED. https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/zpo/__85.html

## 2. Does § 123 PatG use the same due-care standard? (patent cases)

5. BGH X ZB 17/12 (29.10.2013, "Bergbaumaschine"), decided under § 123 Abs. 1 Satz 1 PatG on appeal from BPatG 10 W (pat) 22/09. Rn. 10: a "Rechts- oder Patentanwalt" may delegate computing simple, routine deadlines to a well-trained, tested and supervised employee; clear instructions, clear responsibilities and "zumindest stichprobenartige Kontrolle" are indispensable. SOURCED. https://www.bundesgerichtshof.de/SharedDocs/Entscheidungen/DE/Zivilsenate/X_ZS/2012/X_ZB__17-12.pdf?__blob=publicationFile&v=1
6. X ZB 17/12, Leitsatz b and Rn. 12–13: the standards "vor allem für Rechtsmittelfristen entwickelt" also apply to a patent validation deadline, because missing it loses the right. SOURCED. (same URL)
7. X ZB 17/12, Rn. 14: the many deadlines in patent work may justify that the attorney does not re-check the computation and entry of "aller für das Patent relevanten Fristen" at every file presentation; but no relief from the duty to prevent unauthorised change or deletion of an entered deadline. Rn. 6 reports the BPatG's view that the file-check duty is "eingeschränkt" in patent proceedings. SOURCED. (same URL)
8. X ZB 17/12, Leitsatz a, Rn. 11, 16–21: the attorney must, by general instructions, stop staff from changing or deleting calendar deadlines on their own; a vague instruction to "clarify problems with the attorney" is not sufficient. Rn. 22: a long record without missed deadlines is not proof of adequate organisation. SOURCED. (same URL)
9. BGH X ZR 57/10 (28.09.2010, "Geänderte Berufungsbegründungsfrist", patent nullity case, PatG § 111 and ZPO § 233): where old or new law may govern a deadline, the "Rechtsanwalt oder Patentanwalt" may delegate the computation only with organisational measures that first determine the governing rule. SOURCED (Leitsatz read). https://www.bundesgerichtshof.de/SharedDocs/Entscheidungen/DE/Zivilsenate/X_ZS/2010/X_ZR__57-10.pdf?__blob=publicationFile&v=1
10. BPatG decisions on annual fees and calendars (e.g. 10 W (pat) 16/12, 7 W (pat) 16/17, 26 W (pat) 1/10) apply the same organisational duties. SOURCED (secondary): search extracts only, full texts not read (rewis.io returned HTTP 403). https://rewis.io/urteile/urteil/nzy-17-05-2018-7-w-pat-1617/ ; https://app.omnilex.ai/de/sources/court-decision/de-rii--jure139013444/bpatg-2013-01-21-10-w-pat-1612

## 3. General organisation of Fristenkontrolle (delegation, Vorfrist, Ausgangskontrolle, file check)

11. BGH XII ZB 31/23 (18.10.2023), Rn. 9: errors of office staff do not bar reinstatement unless the lawyer has own fault, such as "Organisations- oder Aufsichtsverschulden". Rn. 12: computation and entry may be delegated to a "gut ausgebildeten, als zuverlässig erprobten und sorgfältig überwachten Bürokraft". Rn. 13: the Handakte must show by Erledigungsvermerk that deadlines are entered in all calendars kept; same for paper or electronic Handakte. Rn. 14: a Vorfrist is required in addition to the main deadline for grounds-of-appeal periods. SOURCED. https://www.bundesgerichtshof.de/SharedDocs/Entscheidungen/DE/Zivilsenate/XII_ZS/2023/XII_ZB__31-23.pdf?__blob=publicationFile&v=1
12. BGH IX ZB 17/18 (18.06.2020), Rn. 9: the lawyer may in principle hand "die Fristen- und Ausgangskontrolle" to office staff; he must ensure a sound organisation, choose staff carefully and check them "durch Stichproben". Rn. 10: when the file is presented to him in connection with the deadline-bound act, he is again personally responsible. SOURCED. https://www.bundesgerichtshof.de/SharedDocs/Entscheidungen/DE/Zivilsenate/IX_ZS/2018/IX_ZB__17-18.pdf?__blob=publicationFile&v=1
13. BGH XII ZB 483/21 (01.03.2023), Leitsatz a/b and Rn. 11: when files are presented for a deadline-bound act, the lawyer must check the grounds-of-appeal deadlines "eigenverantwortlich", regardless of paper or electronic Handakte; the electronic Handakte may not offer lower "Überprüfungssicherheit". SOURCED. https://www.bundesgerichtshof.de/SharedDocs/Entscheidungen/DE/Zivilsenate/XII_ZS/2021/XII_ZB_483-21.pdf?__blob=publicationFile&v=1
14. BGH VI ZB 19/23 (24.06.2025), Leitsatz: the lawyer's duty when files are presented for the notice of appeal extends to the correct entry of the grounds-of-appeal deadline. SOURCED. https://www.bundesgerichtshof.de/SharedDocs/Entscheidungen/DE/Zivilsenate/VI_ZS/2023/VI_ZB__19-23.pdf?__blob=publicationFile&v=1
15. BGH XII ZB 709/13 (09.07.2014), Leitsatz a: an only-electronic Handakte must give the same reliable information on deadlines and "darf keine geringere Überprüfungssicherheit bieten". SOURCED. https://www.bundesgerichtshof.de/SharedDocs/Entscheidungen/DE/Zivilsenate/XII_ZS/2013/XII_ZB_709-13.pdf?__blob=publicationFile&v=1
16. BGH I ZB 34/24 (21.11.2024), Rn. 10: the Ausgangskontrolle must give "einen gestuften Schutz gegen Fristversäumungen". Rn. 11: a deadline may be struck only after the act is really done; staff must confirm "anhand der Akte" that nothing remains. Rn. 13: the lawyer must order an evening check each working day, against the calendar, "durch eine dazu beauftragte Bürokraft"; this is a "nochmalige, selbständige und abschließende Kontrolle"; struck deadlines must stay visible. SOURCED. https://www.bundesgerichtshof.de/SharedDocs/Entscheidungen/DE/Zivilsenate/I_ZS/2024/I_ZB__34-24.pdf?__blob=publicationFile&v=1
17. I ZB 34/24, Rn. 15: the requirements for deadline organisation "sind bekannt"; gaps in the reinstatement statement allow the conclusion that the measures were missing. SOURCED. (same URL)

## 4. Electronic Fristenkalender, Kontrollausdruck, software

18. BGH XI ZB 23/08 and 24/08 (02.02.2010), Leitsatz: organisational fault if the lawyer uses an EDV calendar but does not order that entries be checked by printing the entered items or by an error log ("Fehlerprotokoll"). Rn. 12: the printout catches both "Datenverarbeitungsfehler" and input errors, and can be attached to the document presented to the lawyer. SOURCED. https://www.bundesgerichtshof.de/SharedDocs/Entscheidungen/DE/Zivilsenate/XI_ZS/2008/XI_ZB__23-08.pdf?__blob=publicationFile&v=1
19. XI ZB 23/08, Rn. 11: the court says it is already "zweifelhaft" whether a careful lawyer may use software that enters the two appeal deadlines separately and gives no error message on a wrong service date; left undecided. SOURCED. (same URL)
20. BGH VI ZB 55/11 (17.04.2012), Rn. 8: the same rule (printout of entered items or program error log; else organisational fault). SOURCED. https://www.bundesgerichtshof.de/SharedDocs/Entscheidungen/DE/Zivilsenate/VI_ZS/2011/VI_ZB__55-11.pdf?__blob=publicationFile&v=1
21. BGH V ZB 138/17 (12.04.2018) is cited in III ZB 96/18 (Rn. 13) as confirming the printout rule (NJW-RR 2018, 1267 Rn. 7, 9). SOURCED for the PDF location; its own text I did not read in detail. https://www.bundesgerichtshof.de/SharedDocs/Entscheidungen/DE/Zivilsenate/V_ZS/2017/V_ZB_138-17.pdf?__blob=publicationFile&v=1
22. BGH III ZB 96/18 (28.02.2019), Leitsatz a: entries in the electronic calendar must be checked "durch einen Ausdruck der eingegebenen Einzelvorgänge oder eines Fehlerprotokolls"; otherwise organisational fault. SOURCED. https://www.bundesgerichtshof.de/SharedDocs/Entscheidungen/DE/Zivilsenate/III_ZS/2018/III_ZB__96-18.pdf?__blob=publicationFile&v=1
23. III ZB 96/18, Leitsatz b and Rn. 16–20: a software dialog "Eingabekontrolle" that automatically re-reads the stored deadlines and shows them on screen is "nicht gleich effektiv und sicher" as a paper printout. Reason: a screen-only multi-step check is open to "Augenblicksversagen" (momentary failure) of staff (Rn. 16–18). Rn. 19: a missing printout is a visible warning sign; only the "Medienbruch" between screen entry and printed check gives high security. SOURCED. (same URL)
24. III ZB 96/18, Rn. 15: an "Fehlerprotokoll" as alternative "beinhaltet auch ... die Fertigung eines Ausdrucks"; earlier decisions say "stets ein Schriftstück" (II ZB 33/04) and "in jedem Fall ein Ausdruck" (IX ZB 111/96). SOURCED. (same URL)
25. III ZB 96/18, Rn. 8 (reasoning of the OLG Celle, which the BGH upheld in Rn. 10): only a printout makes sure human errors can be corrected by "eine weitere von einem Mitarbeiter vorgenommene Kontrolle"; digitalisation does not change this. SOURCED. (same URL) Note: this is the lower court's wording, approved but not restated by the BGH.
26. III ZB 96/18, Rn. 11: reinstatement may not be denied by care requirements that the highest-court case law does not demand (constitutional right to effective legal protection). SOURCED. (same URL)
27. BGH X ZB 2/20 (02.02.2021, X. Zivilsenat — the patent senate, but here a ZPO air-passenger case), Rn. 8: a printout is required "grundsätzlich"; placing it in the Handakte lets the lawyer do his own deadline check. Rn. 9: no lower standard during migration from paper to electronic calendar. SOURCED. https://www.bundesgerichtshof.de/SharedDocs/Entscheidungen/DE/Zivilsenate/X_ZS/2020/X_ZB___2-20.pdf?__blob=publicationFile&v=1
28. X ZB 2/20, Rn. 10: whether "andere Maßnahmen" can give an equivalent possibility of own-responsibility deadline control was left undecided. Rn. 11: whether the printout rule still applies with a purely electronic file "bedarf keiner abschließenden Entscheidung". Rn. 13: printouts may be omitted "allenfalls dann", if other measures give "ein vergleichbares Maß an Sicherheit". SOURCED. (same URL)
29. BGH III ZB 82/23 (26.09.2024), Leitsatz and Rn. 9: also with an electronic calendar, a control of the calendar is needed to catch "Datenverarbeitungsfehler des eingesetzten Programms" and input errors. SOURCED. https://www.bundesgerichtshof.de/SharedDocs/Entscheidungen/DE/Zivilsenate/III_ZS/2023/III_ZB__82-23.pdf?__blob=publicationFile&v=1
30. III ZB 82/23, Rn. 10: the appellant asked whether control is sufficient when market-tested software is used and trained staff enter data under the "Vier-Augen-Prinzip". The BGH: already settled against her, because that does not detect the processing error; a lawyer may not assume correct data processing "ohne weiteren Kontrollschritt". The court again left open "ob es eines Kontrollausdrucks in Papierform bedarf". Facts (Rn. 4): software (RA-Micro) automatically assigned the deadline to a wrong lawyer's calendar; two employees had checked the entry. SOURCED. (same URL)
31. BGH XII ZB 338/24 (04.03.2026), Leitsatz a/b and Rn. 11–12: calendars must keep struck and changed deadlines visible and checkable; the same applies to electronic calendars. Rn. 13: if the chosen software cannot show changed deadlines, this does not excuse the lawyer; "Organisationsverschulden bei der Auswahl der Software". Facts (Rn. 2): an employee entered a new start date and the software recalculated all deadlines. SOURCED. https://www.bundesgerichtshof.de/SharedDocs/Entscheidungen/DE/Zivilsenate/XII_ZS/2024/XII_ZB_338-24.pdf?__blob=publicationFile&v=1
32. XII ZB 338/24, Rn. 10: high care is required; computation and entry may be delegated to a reliable, supervised Bürokraft, but the lawyer must ensure deadlines are entered and "kontrolliert". Rn. 16–17: lasting visibility of changes is an extra safety level that works also when other measures (staff instructions) fail. SOURCED. (same URL)

## What the sources do not say

- No decision read requires a second person to check each calendar entry. The standard is: delegation to a trained, tested, supervised clerk is allowed (facts 5, 11, 12, 32), plus an organised control of each electronic entry (printout or error log) (facts 18, 20, 22). Who does the printout check is not fixed by the BGH; III ZB 96/18 Rn. 19 describes the printout as a warning sign to "der mit der Fristeintragung befassten Person". Only the OLG reasoning approved in III ZB 96/18 (fact 25) speaks of a check "von einem Mitarbeiter".
- The "Vier-Augen-Prinzip" appears in III ZB 82/23 only as the appellant's argument; the BGH said two people entering data does not replace a control that catches data-processing errors (fact 30). It did not say four eyes are required.
- No decision read accepts an automated, software-side check in place of a Kontrollausdruck. III ZB 96/18 rejects one specific screen-based software self-check (fact 23). X ZB 2/20 and III ZB 82/23 leave open whether other measures with "vergleichbares Maß an Sicherheit" can replace a paper printout (facts 28, 30).
- No decision read addresses: a structured event source (e.g. an office's machine-readable register data) as the input; two independent software readers; a rule set signed by an attorney; a second independent date source; or AI. None says such a design is sufficient, and none says it is insufficient.
- No decision read removes the lawyer's own duty to check deadlines when the file is presented (facts 12–14). For patent attorneys, X ZB 17/12 Rn. 14 says that duty may be narrower because of the number of deadlines, but not the duty to prevent unauthorised changes (fact 7).
- The decisions concern reinstatement (fault attribution after a missed deadline). They do not state professional-conduct rules (BRAO/PAO) or liability standards as such; those were not researched here.

## Open legal question (for a patent attorney to judge)

Can a firm that confirms certain deadline entries automatically — input from a structured event source or two independent readers, a computation rule version approved once by an attorney, and a match against a second independent date source — show, in a reinstatement application under § 123 PatG or § 233 ZPO, an organisation that gives "ein vergleichbares Maß an Sicherheit" (X ZB 2/20 Rn. 13) to the Kontrollausdruck required by III ZB 96/18, XI ZB 23/08 and VI ZB 55/11? Sub-questions:

1. Does a software cross-check against an independent source count as the "Kontrolle" that catches "Datenverarbeitungsfehler" (III ZB 82/23 Rn. 9–10), or does the BGH expect a human-perceivable step with a "Medienbruch" (III ZB 96/18 Rn. 19)?
2. Does an attorney-signed rule version satisfy the duty to supervise delegated computation (X ZR 57/10; X ZB 17/12 Rn. 10), and how often must it be sample-checked ("stichprobenartig")?
3. How does the design keep changed and struck deadlines visible (XII ZB 338/24) and preserve the attorney's own check on file presentation (XII ZB 483/21; X ZB 17/12 Rn. 14)?
4. Does a software-quality defect become the firm's "Organisationsverschulden bei der Auswahl der Software" (XII ZB 338/24 Rn. 13; XI ZB 23/08 Rn. 11)?
5. Because reinstatement facts must be stated and made credible (§ 123 Abs. 2 PatG; § 236 ZPO; I ZB 34/24 Rn. 15), what audit records must the system keep to prove the control was done for the specific entry?
