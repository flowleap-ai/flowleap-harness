# EPO primary sources for the MyEPO process sheets

Research date: 2026-11-10. Labels: SOURCED (read) = I read the page or PDF text. SOURCED (secondary) = search-result snippet or a non-EPO page only. NOT VERIFIED = page blocked, not rendered, or not found. Quotes are at most 15 words.

## 1. Electronic notification through the MyEPO Mailbox

1. The decision now in force is the Decision of the President of the EPO dated 25 March 2026 concerning the web-based service MyEPO and electronic notification to the Mailbox in proceedings under the EPC and the PCT, OJ EPO 2026, A20. It entered into force on 1 April 2026. SOURCED (read). https://www.epo.org/en/legal/official-journal/2026/03/a20
2. OJ EPO 2026, A20, Art. 2(1): "Electronic notification under Rule 127 EPC is effected by electronic communication" (to an activated Mailbox). Art. 2(2): the document reaches its destination "on the date the document is made available in the corresponding Mailbox". Art. 2(5): no paper copies are notified in parallel. SOURCED (read). Same URL.
3. OJ EPO 2026, A20, Art. 6: the decision of 9 February 2024 (OJ EPO 2024, A20) "will cease to have effect". SOURCED (read). Same URL.
4. OJ EPO 2026, A20 does not mention opening, reading or downloading, Mailbox retention, or email alerts. SOURCED (read). Same URL.
5. Chain of predecessor decisions: OJ EPO 2023, A49 (3 May 2023) was replaced by OJ EPO 2023, A89 (9 October 2023, in force 1 November 2023, Art. 11 and 12), which was replaced by OJ EPO 2024, A20 (9 February 2024, in force 1 April 2024, Art. 7 and 8). SOURCED (read) for A89 and 2024 A20. https://www.epo.org/en/legal/official-journal/2023/10/a89.html and https://www.epo.org/en/legal/official-journal/2024/02/a20
6. OJ EPO 2023, A48 is a separate 3 May 2023 decision on electronic filing, not on notification. SOURCED (secondary, search snippet). https://www.epo.org/xx/legal/official-journal/2023/05/a48/2023-a48.pdf
7. OJ EPO 2025, A27 (24 March 2025, in force 1 April 2025), Art. 2: MyEPO Portfolio "is renamed MyEPO". It does not repeal OJ EPO 2024, A20. SOURCED (read). https://www.epo.org/en/legal/official-journal/2025/03/a27
8. OJ EPO 2023, A89, Art. 6(3): email alerts "are provided as a courtesy service for information only", are not notifications under Rule 127 EPC, failure to send one "will have no effect" on a notification or time limit, and "Users may not invoke the omission of an alert." SOURCED (read). https://www.epo.org/en/legal/official-journal/2023/10/a89.html . Note: this article is not in OJ EPO 2024, A20 or OJ EPO 2026, A20 (see fact 4 and "What the sources do not say").
9. OJ EPO 2023, A89, Art. 2(4): for documents in the Mailbox from 1 November 2023, the document reaches its destination on the date it is made available in the Mailbox. SOURCED (read). Same URL.
10. Notice from the EPO dated 6 March 2023 concerning amended Rules 126, 127 and 131 EPC (OJ EPO 2023, A29): the ten-day rule "will be abandoned from 1 November 2023"; notification is "deemed to occur on the date of the document"; electronic notification "will not occur before the date of the document"; earlier Mailbox access does not change "the decisive date for the purpose of the notification fiction". SOURCED (read). https://www.epo.org/en/legal/official-journal/2023/03/a29
11. The same notice, safeguard: if the EPO cannot show receipt "within seven days of the date it bears", the triggered period is extended by the days beyond seven (example: receipt on day 12 gives 5 extra days). The EPO has the burden of proof if receipt is disputed. SOURCED (read). Same URL.
12. Rule 127(2) EPC (in force from 1.11.2023, CA/D 10/22; later wording CA/D 20/24 from 1.4.2025): the electronic document is deemed delivered on the date it bears, unless it failed to reach its destination; seven-day extension as in fact 11. The epo.org page showed old and new wording mixed, so read the official text before quoting. SOURCED (read, with that caution). https://www.epo.org/en/legal/epc/2020/r127.html
13. Rule 126(2) EPC (postal) has the same date-it-bears rule and seven-day extension from 1.11.2023 (CA/D 10/22, OJ EPO 2022, A101). SOURCED (read). https://www.epo.org/en/legal/epc/2020/r126.html
14. Guidelines 2026, E-II, 2.3 "Electronic notification": the document is "deemed to be delivered to them on the date it bears"; if contested, "it is incumbent on the EPO to establish that it has reached its destination"; seven-day safeguard applies. Documents are made available in the Mailbox reachable through MyEPO. SOURCED (read). https://www.epo.org/en/legal/guidelines-epc/2026/e_ii_2_3.html
15. FUTURE CHANGE. Decision of the Administrative Council CA/D 2/26 of 19 March 2026 (OJ EPO 2026, A27) deletes Rules 126, 127 and 128 EPC and amends Rule 125 EPC, with effect from 1 April 2027. New Rule 125(2): electronic notification is the default; the document is "deemed to be delivered to the addressee on the date it bears" with the same seven-day extension. New Rule 125(3): postal notification only in special circumstances. New Rule 125(5): if notification cannot be proved, the date of receipt established by the EPO applies. Transitional: amended Rules 125 and 129 apply to documents dated 1 April 2027 or later (Art. 3(2)). SOURCED (read). https://www.epo.org/en/legal/official-journal/2026/04/a27
16. Mailbox retention: "Communications are not automatically deleted" from the Inbox or other folders; "The Deleted folder empties automatically after 28 days". SOURCED (read). https://epo.org/en/service-support/faq/myepo-services/myepo/mailbox/are-communications-automatically-deleted-after

## 2. MyEPO Mailbox, Portfolio and API guidance

17. Feature guide "Handling Mailbox communications" (last updated March 2025): the Mailbox is "a company-based service"; communications go to a company Mailbox, not to individuals. SOURCED (read). https://link.epo.org/web/myepo-services/interact/en-myepo-feature-guide-handling-mailbox-communications.pdf
18. Same guide: the company administrator adds "recipients" (European representative, legal practitioner, Rule 156(11) association, or self-representing party in an EPC state); the EPO checks each request ("several working days"); the administrator sets Electronic communications to "Yes" per recipient and activates the Mailbox. A new company Mailbox is initially deactivated. SOURCED (read). Same URL.
19. Same guide: the administrator chooses in Admin > Company members which users can handle Mailbox communications. SOURCED (read). Same URL.
20. Same guide: the Mailbox has standard folders "(Inbox, Unread, etc)" and user-created folders. Users can mark communications as "handled", move them to a folder, delete them, download a CSV list, or download files as ZIP. SOURCED (read). Same URL.
21. Same guide, email alerts: "New communications" is for Mailbox users only; "New procedural actions" and "Upcoming deadlines" are only for users with portfolio access, and apply only to actions "currently available in the Actions screen". SOURCED (read). Same URL.
22. "Not handled" view: it holds communications not yet marked handled "by one of the Mailbox users"; marking removes it from that view (so handled status is company-wide). The Mailbox records viewing or downloading, marking as handled, and moving to a folder. SOURCED (secondary, search snippet from EPO user guide/FAQ). https://link.epo.org/web/MyEPO_Portfolio_user_guide_en.pdf and https://epo.org/en/service-support/faq/myepo-services/myepo/mailbox
23. Feature guide "APIs for exchanging information with your IP management systems" (last update March 2025): the MyEPO API can "retrieve lists of Mailbox communications (handled, unhandled) and lists of folders", download communications (documents and data), "move Mailbox communications into a folder and mark as handled", retrieve due fees, "retrieve due dates for an application and descriptions for the terms", retrieve applications and portfolios, bibliographic data, the fee schedule, and update user references. SOURCED (read). https://link.epo.org/web/understand-our-services/exchange-data-with-us-using-an-api/en-myepo-feature-guide-apis-for-exchanging-information-with-ip-management-systems.pdf
24. Same guide: a sandbox with dummy data; sandbox credentials on request through epo.org/support; OAuth 2.0 client ID and client secret. SOURCED (read). Same URL.
25. Same guide, production: the company must already use MyEPO with at least one user and one company administrator; the EPO creates a "B2B (business-to-business) account" and sends the OAuth 2.0 credentials to the company administrator; the administrator must grant it Mailbox access rights, and permissions to the portfolios; without portfolio permission the API cannot read or update those applications. Credentials must not be shared with people who should not see confidential company data. SOURCED (read). Same URL.
26. Same guide: APIs follow WIPO Standard ST.90. Vendors are mentioned only as "your IP management software supplier". SOURCED (read). Same URL and https://www.epo.org/en/applying/myepo-services/understand/api
27. API page links: Developer Portal https://api.apps.epo.org/dev-portal/ ; MyEPO API docs https://api.apps.epo.org/dev-portal/api-docs?spec=myepo-prod ; FAQ https://www.epo.org/en/service-support/faq/myepo-services/apis-exchanging-information-your-it-systems ; release notes. No API-specific terms, rate limits or disclaimer on the page. SOURCED (read). https://www.epo.org/en/applying/myepo-services/understand/api
28. API release notes: 29 April 2024 (1.14.0) due fees endpoint; 28 June 2024 (1.16.0) endpoints to "retrieve due dates for an application", retrieve terms, due fees (old due-fees endpoint deprecated); 2 October 2024 (1.17.0) applications, portfolios, bibliographic data, fee schedule, user references; 21 February 2025 Developer Portal launch. No mention of read status, webhooks or rate limits. SOURCED (read). https://www.epo.org/en/applying/myepo-services/understand/api/release-notes
29. Feature guide "replying to communications with deadlines": the Actions area shows pending replies with deadlines for Rules 62a/63, Rule 70a, Art. 94(3), Rule 71(3) and Rules 161(1)/162 (161/162 not auto-listed "because in some cases it is voluntary"). Replies signed by name and function of an authorised person. A reply made in MyEPO goes into the digital file. SOURCED (read). https://link.epo.org/web/myepo-services/interact/en-myepo-feature-guide-replying-to-communications-with-deadlines.pdf
30. MyEPO FAQ: "Currently MyEPO only shows procedural actions and deadlines for the following replies" (Rule 71(3), Art. 94(3), Rule 70a, Rules 62a/63). So MyEPO deadlines are not a complete docket. SOURCED (read). https://epo.org/en/service-support/faq/myepo-services/myepo/applications-and-patents/one-my-applications-theres
31. MyEPO FAQ: "Upcoming deadlines" sends an alert on each of the 14 days before a deadline; an action done by other means "will remain pending" until a formalities officer processes it, so alerts can arrive after the act was done. SOURCED (read). https://www.epo.org/en/service-support/faq/myepo-services/myepo/preferences-email-alerts/i-received-email-alert-although

## 3. Disclaimers, EP Register, Register Alert

32. No EPO page read states that MyEPO deadlines or due dates are "for information only" or legally non-binding. The only such statement found is for email alerts (fact 8, OJ EPO 2023, A89, Art. 6(3)), and it is absent from the later decisions. SOURCED (read, absence).
33. EPO "Disclaimer" page (useful links): EPO "makes no warranty, either express or implied, as to the accuracy" and no implied responsibility; context appears to be linked or external information, not MyEPO deadlines. SOURCED (read). https://epo.org/en/service-support/useful-links/disclaimer
34. Register FAQ: legal information is given "for proceedings before the EPO only"; national-phase and INPADOC data are supplementary and carry disclaimers; legal basis Art. 127 EPC and Rule 143(1) EPC. SOURCED (read). https://www.epo.org/en/service-support/faq/searching-patents/european-patent-register-and-federated-register/european-5
35. Register FAQ: "Publicly available outgoing communications are displayed in the Register" the day after despatch; incoming ones "as a rule one day after the document has been coded"; the date in EP/UP All documents "is the date of receipt by the EPO" (stated for incoming documents). SOURCED (read). https://www.epo.org/en/service-support/faq/searching-patents/european-patent-register-and-federated-register/european-4
36. Register Alert FAQ: alerts for outgoing communications are sent once the events "are coded by the EPO"; same for incoming. Frequency not stated. SOURCED (read). https://www.epo.org/en/service-support/faq/searching-patents/european-patent-register-and-federated-register/register-1
37. Register events page: a Register event is a procedural action that involves coding and "can trigger an entry" in the Register; it shows as "Most recent event" in About this file and in Event history; "Certain procedural actions do not trigger an event"; events trigger Register Alert emails. SOURCED (read). https://epo.org/en/searching-for-patents/legal/register/documentation/events
38. The full list of Register events (names such as despatch of examination communication) is NOT VERIFIED: the page returned HTTP 403. https://register.epo.org/allEvents?lng=en
39. Lost-letter check (inference, not an EPO statement): facts 35 and 37 support comparing outgoing documents shown in the public Register (day after despatch) with Mailbox items. Limits: unpublished applications are not public; some actions create no event. Inference from SOURCED facts.

## 5. Grant procedure (Guidelines 2026)

43. C-V, 1.1: the Rule 71(3) communication sets a "non-extendable period of four months" to pay the fee for grant and publishing and file claim translations in the two other official languages; doing so without requesting amendments means approval of the text (Rule 71(5)). SOURCED (read). https://www.epo.org/en/legal/guidelines-epc/2026/c_v_1_1.html
44. C-V, 3: if the acts are not done in time, the application is "deemed withdrawn"; Rule 71(7) allows further processing under Art. 121. SOURCED (read). https://www.epo.org/en/legal/guidelines-epc/2026/c_v_3.html
45. E-VIII, 2: further processing must be requested "within two months of the communication" on the missed time limit or loss of rights, with the omitted act; for the Rule 71(3) payments one flat fee applies (late claims fees add 50%). SOURCED (read). https://www.epo.org/en/legal/guidelines-epc/2026/e_viii_2.html
46. C-V, 2: the decision to grant issues when C-V 1.1 to 1.4 are met and due renewal fees are paid; Rule 71a(1) holds the decision if a renewal fee falls due before publication; the decision "does not take effect until the date on which the grant is mentioned in the European Patent Bulletin" (Art. 97(3)). SOURCED (read). https://www.epo.org/en/legal/guidelines-epc/2026/c_v_2.html

## What the sources do not say

- No source states a retention limit for documents in the Inbox or user folders (only the 28-day Deleted folder).
- No source says that opening, reading or downloading a document changes the notification date. All sources tie notification to the date the document bears (or is made available), and OJ EPO 2023, A29 says earlier access does not change it.
- OJ EPO 2026, A20 and OJ EPO 2024, A20 do not repeat the email-alert courtesy clause of OJ EPO 2023, A89, Art. 6. Whether that rule now lives elsewhere was not found.
- No EPO page read says MyEPO due dates or deadlines are informative only, non-binding, or that the EPO accepts no liability for them. The FAQ only says the deadline coverage is limited.
- No source read gives API rate limits, quotas, polling rules, webhooks, or API terms of use for vendors. The API docs pages render with JavaScript and showed no such text (NOT VERIFIED).
- No source read says whether the API exposes or sets a per-communication "read" status. The API guide names "handled, unhandled" and folders only; the UI has an "Unread" folder.
- The EP Register event list (register.epo.org/allEvents) returned HTTP 403 and was not read. The meaning of Event history dates for outgoing communications is not stated in the FAQs read.
- The document code list beyond code 3034 was not read; 2006A and Art. 94(3)-specific codes are not confirmed.
