# FlowLeap Patent AI: version 1 scope

Status: written on 2026-11-10 from the founder's decisions of that day. It replaces [the v1 scope of 2026-10-28](2026-10-28-v1-scope.md). Check every new idea against this page. An idea that is not in "v1 supports" is not in v1. The acceptance test of v1 is [the v1 user stories](../qa/v1/README.md).

## The test of v1

A firm runs its EP prosecution on FlowLeap for one month, beside its old docket, and trusts what it sees.

v1 is a workable product, not an MVP. v1 passes when every story in [the v1 user stories](../qa/v1/README.md) passes by [the pass rule](../qa/v1/how-to-run-a-qa-round.md#when-v1-passes). After that, the work moves to UX and UI improvements.

## v1 supports

Each line is a capability that a person of the firm can see. The id (V1 to V25) is the key of [the coverage table](../qa/v1/coverage.md).

**The organisation**

- **V1.** The firm administrator sets the firm identity, the persons and their roles, and the firm settings, in one place.
- **V2.** Each act needs its role: administrator, partner, attorney, formalities. A person who does not have the role cannot do the act.
- **V3.** Walls: a person sees only the matters of their client group or matter team, and never a matter that screens them.
- **V4.** Firm state is in one place: a fact of the firm is in the Firm Record or a firm service. When one person changes it, every other person sees the change. An app instance keeps only the person's own state.

**The firm connectors**

- **V5.** MyEPO, linked by the administrator for the whole firm.
- **V6.** EPO OPS, linked by the administrator for the whole firm.

**Getting the firm's matters in**

- **V7.** Import your EP docket.
- **V8.** Add client.

**Letters and deadlines**

- **V9.** Letters from the EPO Mailbox arrive by themselves and are filed on their matter automatically.
- **V10.** The read flag: a download of a Mailbox letter marks it as read in the firm's MyEPO. A firm setting decides when FlowLeap downloads. The default is "download when a person or a task opens the letter".
- **V11.** Deadlines are computed for the most frequent EP terms.
- **V12.** Deadline confirmation. The default level is strict: a person confirms each deadline, and the person who entered it cannot confirm it. Confirmation by algorithm runs in shadow mode: a deadline is confirmed automatically only when its event comes from a structured source, its rule version is signed by an attorney, and a second independent source agrees. Otherwise a person gets it, with the reason.

**The four surfaces**

- **V13.** My work: what waits for me, and the decisions that are mine.
- **V14.** Letters: every office letter of the firm, inside the walls.
- **V15.** Deadlines: every deadline of the firm, inside the walls.
- **V16.** The matter page with its files.

**The agent**

- **V17.** Matter memory: a matter's letters, register events, files and client instructions, each with its source.
- **V18.** Prepared work for two letter types: the agent prepares and checks, the attorney decides and writes.

**Notices**

- **V19.** Notices to Outlook.

**Already built and kept**

- **V20.** Sign-in with Microsoft Entra.
- **V21.** One runtime for each attorney.
- **V22.** The model gateway, with a budget for each attorney.
- **V23.** Backups, with a tested restore.
- **V24.** Updates, with automatic rollback.
- **V25.** The release gate: no candidate reaches a firm before it passes on the local firm.

## Decisions for the founder

Each item is necessary for the test of v1 or for a story, and is not settled by the founder's list. Each has a recommendation. The stories follow the recommendation and name the decision.

1. **Sign-out and the account switch on a shared PC.** Not on the list. Without them, the next person on a shared PC works as the previous person (QA findings 1 and 39). Recommendation: in v1, as part of V20.
2. **Find a matter.** Not on the list. A person cannot work for a month without a search (QA finding 15). Recommendation: in v1, inside the walls, as part of the four surfaces (os `GET search` is built; the screen is in PR 346).
3. **Enter the office event and the due date by hand.** Not on the list. The engine has one rule, and Art. 94(3), Rule 79(1) and loss-of-rights letters have no rule, so without hand entry those letters get no deadline. Recommendation: in v1, with the second-person confirm (os built; screen in PR 346).
4. **"Filed automatically" against `intake.exactKeyFiles: person-confirms`.** The merged sheet 01 and the os settings let a person confirm each filing until a shadow period shows no wrong filing. Recommendation: in v1, file automatically only on an exact application-number match in the MyEPO list data; every other case goes to a person.
5. **The "most frequent EP terms".** Not named. Recommendation: Rule 71(3), Art. 94(3), Rule 70(2) and 70a(2), and Rule 161(1) and 162; every other term by hand entry (decision 3).
6. **The two letter types of prepared work.** Not named. Recommendation: Rule 71(3) and Art. 94(3), as in roadmap step M3.
7. **The lost-letter check.** A register event with no letter in the Mailbox. The month beside the old docket fails if one letter is lost and nobody sees it. Recommendation: in v1, on the firm's OPS link (V6), as S16 draws it for sheet 01.
8. **The pass rule for a deadline in the month.** Recommendation: a FlowLeap deadline passes when its date equals the old docket's date, or when a person recorded the reason for the difference. The Deadlines surface must give a list that a person can put beside the old docket (printed or exported; PATORG cross-check item P22).
9. **A person who is absent.** Deputies and absences are #253 (R3). Recommendation: v1 has the escalation by timer only (built), and the stories test that; deputies stay in R3.
10. **Client instructions in the matter memory.** Recommendation: in v1, a person records an instruction on the client or the matter with its source mail attached; reading the instructions from Outlook stays in M1.
11. **The forwarded-mail route and OCR.** Both exist. Recommendation: the forward stays as the fallback route in v1; OCR of scans is not a v1 line.
12. **The firm role on admin acts in the Firm Record.** A defect, not a choice: an attorney can do firm-level acts through the record API (V2). Recommendation: a fix before the QA round. The ticket is not filed yet: its text waits for the planning session (title "Firm Record: firm-level commands check the sender kind but not the person's firm role").
13. **Open questions of the work in progress.** These belong to S16 and the mockup review, not to this page. The stories test the founder's decision of 2026-11-10 and mark each one:
    - S16 downloads letters that set their own period at arrival in both read flag modes (`myepo.downloadAtArrivalCodes`), so MyEPO shows them as read at once. Recommendation: accept only if S16 shows that the period needs the PDF; else keep the founder's default with no exception.
    - S16 counts a two-reader model reading as a structured source. Recommendation: no; only the MyEPO list data and the register count, by the founder's words.
    - PR 388 draws "Save anyway" for a MyEPO link test that times out; S16 refuses it (D6.4). Recommendation: no "Save anyway".
    - PR 388 shows a deadline "confirmed automatically" while the default level is strict. Recommendation: remove it from the default state of the mockup.
14. **The main customer type.** Still open. The install stories test the self-hosted firm server, because that is what exists.
15. **The end of a deadline: met and replaced.** Sheet 02 puts `active` to `to_docket` (met, superseded) in R2. Without it, every deadline whose act was done stays overdue, and the daily comparison with the old docket (MON-02) differs on almost every line. Recommendation: in v1, a person closes a deadline as met or replaced, with its evidence (DL-21, DL-22).
16. **A changed date of an active deadline.** An EPO extension under Rule 132(2) moves an active date; sheet 02 does not track it in R1. Recommendation: in v1, by hand, with the letter as evidence and a second person (DL-28).
17. **Settings that weaken a check.** The confirmation level, the confirmer roles and the intake persons decide the firm's duty of care. Recommendation: the administrator proposes a weaker value and a partner approves it, and every partner gets a notice (NEV-28).
18. **An unreadable scan and a screened intake person.** A scan with no text has no proposed matter, so a screened intake person can read it (os `test/record-unfiled-letters.test.mjs`). Recommendation: accept this as a recorded limit of the forward route in v1, because MyEPO letters carry their application number; the partner gets a wall incident when a screened person opens such a scan (LET-22).
19. **Who sets the client groups and matter teams.** Walls from Entra groups are #227, not in v1, and no screen sets a team. Recommendation: a partner or the responsible attorney, recorded with the actor; the administrator gives roles only (ADM-20).
20. **Reminders before the due date.** `deadline.internalDates` (2 weeks and 1 week before) is in sheet 02, not in the code. A German firm expects a Vorfrist for each deadline. Recommendation: in v1 (DL-24).
