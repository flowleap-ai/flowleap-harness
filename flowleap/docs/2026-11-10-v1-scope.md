# FlowLeap Patent AI: version 1 scope

Status: **approved by the founder on 2026-11-10**, with the eight additions V26 to V31, V33 and V34, and decision A (V32). It replaces [the v1 scope of 2026-10-28](2026-10-28-v1-scope.md). Check every new idea against this page. An idea that is not in "v1 supports" is not in v1. The acceptance test of v1 is [the v1 user stories](../qa/v1/README.md).

## The test of v1

A firm runs its EP prosecution on FlowLeap for one month, beside its old docket, and trusts what it sees.

v1 is a workable product, not an MVP. v1 passes when every story in [the v1 user stories](../qa/v1/README.md) passes by [the pass rule](../qa/v1/how-to-run-a-qa-round.md#when-v1-passes). After that, the work moves to UX and UI improvements.

## v1 supports

Each line is a capability that a person of the firm can see. The id (V1 to V34) is the key of [the coverage table](../qa/v1/coverage.md).

**The organisation**

- **V1.** The firm administrator sets the firm identity, the persons and their roles, and the firm settings, in one place.
- **V2.** Each act needs its role: administrator, partner, attorney, formalities. A person who does not have the role cannot do the act.
- **V3.** Walls: a person sees only the matters of their client group or matter team, and never a matter that screens them.
- **V4.** Firm state is in one place: a fact of the firm is in the Firm Record or a firm service. When one person changes it, every other person sees the change. An app instance keeps only the person's own state.
- **V26.** Sign-out and the account switch: on a shared PC, the next person signs in with their own account and never sees the previous person's pages.

**The firm connectors**

- **V5.** MyEPO, linked by the administrator for the whole firm.
- **V6.** EPO OPS, linked by the administrator for the whole firm.

**Getting the firm's matters in**

- **V7.** Import your EP docket.
- **V8.** Add client.

**Letters and deadlines**

- **V9.** Letters from the EPO Mailbox arrive by themselves. A letter is filed on its matter automatically only when the MyEPO list data has an exact application-number match; every other letter goes to a person.
- **V10.** The read flag: a download of a Mailbox letter marks it as read in the firm's MyEPO. A firm setting decides when FlowLeap downloads. The default is "download when a person or a task opens the letter". The one exception is V32 step 2: when MyEPO gives no due date for a letter whose period is only in its text, FlowLeap downloads it at arrival.
- **V11.** Deadlines are computed for the most frequent EP terms: Rule 71(3), Art. 94(3), Rule 70a, and Rules 62a and 63 (sheet 08 decision D8.7). Rules 161 and 162 and renewal fees are entered by hand (V27) in v1.
- **V12.** Deadline confirmation. v1 runs the level `strict` (sheet 08 D8.9): a person confirms each deadline, and the person who entered it cannot confirm it. Confirmation by algorithm runs in shadow mode only: it records what it would do (a deadline whose event comes from a structured source, whose rule version an attorney signed, and that a second independent source agrees with), and a person still confirms. No deadline shows "confirmed automatically" in v1. The level `evidence` is off in v1.
- **V27.** Hand entry: a person enters an office event and its due date by hand, and a second person confirms it.
- **V28.** The lost-letter check: a register event with no letter in the Mailbox gives a person a task.
- **V29.** A person closes a deadline as met or replaced, with its evidence.
- **V30.** A person changes the date of an active deadline for an EPO extension, by hand, with the letter as evidence, and a second person confirms it.
- **V31.** Reminders before the due date.
- **V32.** The deadline of a letter whose period is only in its text (for example Art. 94(3)) comes from three sources, in this order. Each source is used only if the one before gives nothing. (1) The EPO's own due date, which FlowLeap reads from MyEPO for the application at arrival, with no download of the letter. FlowLeap marks it "from the EPO, not yet checked against the letter", and it drives the alarms. (2) The text of the PDF, when a person or a task opens the letter, or at arrival if (1) gave nothing. OCR runs first when the PDF is a scan. (3) A person enters the period by hand, and a second person confirms it (V27). When the letter is read after (1), FlowLeap compares its period with the EPO's date, and a difference goes to two persons. The letter stays the legal source; the EPO's date is provisional. Source (1) is not proved: the MyEPO sandbox has placeholder due dates.

**The four surfaces**

- **V13.** My work: what waits for me, and the decisions that are mine.
- **V14.** Letters: every office letter of the firm, inside the walls.
- **V15.** Deadlines: every deadline of the firm, inside the walls.
- **V16.** The matter page with its files.
- **V33.** Find a matter, inside the walls.

**The agent**

- **V17.** Matter memory: a matter's letters, register events, files and client instructions, each with its source.
- **V18.** Prepared work for two letter types, Rule 71(3) and Art. 94(3): the agent prepares and checks, the attorney decides and writes.
- **V34.** A person records a client instruction on the client or the matter, with its source mail.

**Notices**

- **V19.** Notices to Outlook.

**Already built and kept**

- **V20.** Sign-in with Microsoft Entra.
- **V21.** One runtime for each attorney.
- **V22.** The model gateway, with a budget for each attorney.
- **V23.** Backups, with a tested restore.
- **V24.** Updates, with automatic rollback.
- **V25.** The release gate: no candidate reaches a firm before it passes on the local firm.

## Decisions of the founder

The founder decided each item on 2026-11-10 ("yes accept" for this page with the eight additions; "accept" for decision A). The planning session settled items 4 to 6, 8, 9, 11, 13, 17 to 19 from the founder's decisions of that day and told the founder. The numbers stay, because the stories name them.

1. **Sign-out and the account switch on a shared PC.** Decided 2026-11-10: in v1, as line V26 (QA findings 1 and 39).
2. **Find a matter.** Decided 2026-11-10: in v1, inside the walls, as line V33.
3. **Enter the office event and the due date by hand.** Decided 2026-11-10: in v1, with a second person, as line V27.
4. **Automatic filing.** Decided 2026-11-10: FlowLeap files automatically only on an exact application-number match in the MyEPO list data; every other case goes to a person (V9).
5. **The most frequent EP terms.** Decided 2026-11-10: the terms of sheet 08 decision D8.7, which are Rule 71(3), Art. 94(3), Rule 70a, and Rules 62a and 63. Rules 161 and 162 and renewal fees are entered by hand for now (V11, V27). The sheet replaces the earlier recommendation of this page.
6. **The two letter types of prepared work.** Decided 2026-11-10: Rule 71(3) and Art. 94(3) (V18).
7. **The lost-letter check.** Decided 2026-11-10: in v1, as line V28.
8. **The pass rule for a deadline in the month.** Decided 2026-11-10, as recommended: a FlowLeap deadline passes when its date equals the old docket's date, or when a person recorded the reason for the difference. The Deadlines surface gives a list that a person can put beside the old docket (printed or exported; PATORG cross-check item P22).
9. **A person who is absent.** Decided 2026-11-10, as recommended: v1 has the escalation by timer only; deputies and absences stay in R3 (#253).
10. **Client instructions in the matter memory.** Decided 2026-11-10: in v1, a person records an instruction on the client or the matter with its source mail, as line V34. Reading the instructions from Outlook stays in M1.
11. **The forwarded-mail route and OCR.** Decided 2026-11-10, as recommended: the forward stays as the fallback route in v1, and OCR is not a line of its own. Decision A uses OCR for a scanned MyEPO letter (V32 step 2).
12. **The firm role on admin acts in the Firm Record.** A defect, not a choice: an attorney can do firm-level acts through the record API (V2). Filed as [#394](https://github.com/flowleap-ai/flowleap-harness/issues/394); related to #389. It must be fixed before the QA round.
13. **Open questions of the work in progress.** Decided 2026-11-10:
    - A MyEPO link test that times out has no "Save anyway" (sheet 06 D6.4).
    - A deadline "confirmed automatically" is not a default state: v1 runs `strict` with shadow mode (sheet 08 D8.9).
    - Whether a two-reader reading counts as a structured source is as sheet 08 states it. It matters only at the level `evidence`, which is off in v1.
    - `myepo.downloadAtArrivalCodes` is replaced by decision A. Sheets 01 and 02 still name it; they need an update to decision A. This page does not edit them.
14. **The main customer type.** Soft agreement on 2026-11-10 (the founder's answer was "okay"): the main customer of v1 is the large, self-hosted firm. A single-user install is a firm of one.
15. **The end of a deadline: met and replaced.** Decided 2026-11-10: in v1, as line V29 (DL-21, DL-22).
16. **A changed date of an active deadline.** Decided 2026-11-10: in v1, by hand, with the letter as evidence and a second person, as line V30 (DL-28).
17. **Settings that weaken a check.** Decided 2026-11-10, as recommended, for the confirmer roles and the intake persons: the administrator proposes a weaker value, a partner approves it, and every partner gets a notice (NEV-28). The confirmation level is not an administrator's setting: a partner sets it with a reason (sheet 08 D8.2).
18. **An unreadable scan and a screened intake person.** Decided 2026-11-10, as recommended: a recorded limit of the forward route in v1; the partner gets a wall incident when a screened person opens such a scan (LET-22).
19. **Who sets the matter teams.** Decided 2026-11-10, as recommended: a partner or the responsible attorney, recorded with the actor; the administrator gives roles only (ADM-20). Client groups are not designed (the design leaves client walls as future work). The design's decision D6 lets a formalities person, an attorney or a partner open a matter; who changes a team later is not in D6.
20. **Reminders before the due date.** Decided 2026-11-10: in v1, as line V31 (DL-24).

**Decision A. The deadline of a letter whose period is only in its text.** Decided by the founder on 2026-11-10 ("accept"), as line V32. It replaces the open point about `myepo.downloadAtArrivalCodes` of sheets 01 and 02 (decision D1.2 of sheet 01). The approved sheets do not yet agree with it, and this page does not edit them. Sheet 02 says that the EPO's due date never replaces the engine, and sheet 08 starts a deadline's evidence with an engine or a person's date, so a deadline from the EPO's date alone does not fit sheet 08. A download at arrival in the mode `on-open` breaks the invariant `myepo.download-by-mode` of sheet 06 unless sheet 01 adds it. Sheet 08 does not name OCR as a reader. Sheets 01, 02, 06 and 08 need an amendment that the founder approves before V32 is built.

**Decision B. The first screen and the sidebar.** Decided by the founder on 2026-11-10, after a local test: the sidebar rows are in groups with labels. Work has My work, Letters and Deadlines. Firm has Clients and Matters. Agent has New chat, Chats, Agents and Automation. Setup is at the bottom, with Settings, and Firm admin for administrators only. My work is the one start (INS-12).
