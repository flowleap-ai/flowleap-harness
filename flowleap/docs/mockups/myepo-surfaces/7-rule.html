Rule 71(3) EPC, version 1

The reply to the communication under Rule 71(3) EPC: approve the text for grant, pay the fees and file the claim translations.

In forceFor letters dated from 1 Nov 2023
FlowLeap reviewReviewed by FlowLeap’s patent attorney Example premise
Deadlines computed7

The rule in plain words

  1. 1
    The letter is delivered on the date it bears.
    Rule 126(2) EPC · Rule 127(2) EPC · OJ EPO 2023, A29
  2. 2
    The period starts on the next day.
    Rule 131(2) EPC
  3. 3
    It ends four months later, on the day with the same number. When that month has no such day, it ends on the last day of the month.
    Rule 71(3) EPC · Rule 131(4) EPC
  4. 4
    When an EPO filing office is closed on that day, the period ends on the next day on which all of them are open.
    Rule 134(1) EPC · the EPO notice of the closing days of each year
  • FlowLeap gives a note, not another date, when the letter arrived later than Rule 126(2) EPC allows. A person checks that case.
  • The rule does not compute further processing, a disruption of the EPO services, or an extension after a failure of electronic filing. A person checks these.
