Decisions for the founder

The background did not decide these points. Each mockup shows the default; tell us the other choice where you want it.

  1. 1. The letter states

    Each row shows one of six words: Listed, Filed on its matter, Deadline computed, Deadline confirmed, Done, Needs a person. Opened is a column of its own (PDF), because in both modes FlowLeap files the letter and computes its deadline from the list data before anybody opens the PDF. The words are not atlas states yet.

  2. 2. What done means for a letter

    Default: its deadline is closed (the act is recorded, a later letter replaced it, or the EP Register shows the next step), or the letter starts no period.

  3. 3. The names of the confirmation levels

    Default: Automatic, Confirmed by a named person, and Needs a person with its reason (sources differ, no rule, entered by hand, rule not signed). Decision 6 is not a process sheet yet; every confirmation part carries the mark “Design not yet approved”.

  4. 4. Today every deadline goes to a person

    No attorney has signed a rule version of the FlowLeap deadline rules. The automatic confirmation of SF-2023-0045 assumes that Dr. Martin Krüger signed Rule 71(3) EPC, version 1; the page marks it as an example premise. Who may sign a rule version is open.

  5. 5. The import steps

    Default: the Add client stepper with four steps (Docket, Clients, Conflict check, Review) and Record. Role is a column of the Clients step with the default Client, not a step, because every proposed client comes from your own applications.

  6. 6. How FlowLeap groups applicant names

    Default: names that differ only in capital letters, punctuation or the legal form become one proposed client; two companies stay apart (Siemens Aktiengesellschaft and Siemens Healthineers AG). A person can split or join rows.

  7. 7. Who runs the import

    Default: a partner, because the import records every client of the firm. The mockups show Dr. Martin Krüger.

  8. 8. A letter without an application number

    Default: the persons who file the firm’s letters read it, as for any letter that is not filed (a security invariant of the Firm Record). The mockups make Dr. Anna Becker one of them (example).

  9. 9. No count outside the wall

    Letters and Deadlines show no count of the items outside a person’s teams, because the Firm Record gives a person outside a matter’s team no row, no search hit and no count of it (security invariants of the Firm Record). Counts outside the walls for a partner (atlas decision Q10) are later.

  10. 10. The console card

    Default: Test and save tests the credentials before it saves them; the EPO refusal saves nothing; when the EPO does not answer, Save anyway saves without a test, as the console does for a provider key. Replace credentials and Unlink stay on the linked card. Linked by and the last read of the Mailbox are facts on the card.

  11. 11. Where the console does not meet the design language

    Not redesigned here. The console has its own stylesheet: literal colours instead of client tokens, a 15 px system font and weight 600, 1 px borders, a 4 px state bar on each key card, uppercase labels with letter spacing, a solid primary button on every card (the design language allows one), no dark theme, and no 390 px layout. The mockup draws the console’s parts with client tokens.

  12. 12. Which Outlook calendar

    Default: each confirmed deadline goes to the Outlook calendar of its responsible attorney. The other choice is one shared firm calendar. A deadline that is not confirmed does not go to Outlook.

  13. 13. A deadline with no date in the table

    Default: “No date yet” sorts before every date, because a person must enter it first.

  14. 14. The month view

    Default: it opens at the current month, shows the overdue deadlines and those with no date as two chips above the month, and becomes a list of days on a phone. The mockup shows January 2027, the month of the demo deadline.

  15. 15. The download setting

    Default: on a firm installation it is on the console card, and only the administrator changes it; the app card shows the mode as a fact. On a single-user installation it stays on the app card. A change applies from the next letter. Prepared work exists only after a download, so in the default mode the agent prepares the work when a person opens the letter.

  16. 16. My work counts letters

    Default: the first line of My work counts a MyEPO letter as arrived and a letter with prepared work as worked, as the My work of PR 343 does.

Files

Files is the Files tab of the matter page, which exists (client model mockups, approved on 9 Nov 2026). FlowLeap files each letter on its matter by its application number, in the folder Office, when a person or a task opens it. The letter page shows where. There is no file manager.
