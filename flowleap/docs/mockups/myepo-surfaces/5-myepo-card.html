Letters from the Mailbox

A download marks a letter as read in your firm’s MyEPO. Choose when FlowLeap downloads.

Download a letter when a person or a task opens it
Default. For the time beside your old docket: MyEPO stays as your team knows it.
  • In your MyEPO, a letter stays unread until a person or a task opens it in FlowLeap.
  • FlowLeap files each letter and computes its deadline at arrival, from the list data.
  • The agent prepares the work on a letter only after a person opens it.
Download each letter when it arrives
The agent prepares the work before anybody opens the letter.
  • In your MyEPO, every letter shows as read a few minutes after it arrives. A person who works in MyEPO no longer sees which letters are new.
  • FlowLeap files each letter and computes its deadline at arrival, from the list data.
  • The agent reads the PDF and prepares the work at once.

In both modes, FlowLeap never marks a letter as handled in MyEPO.
