## Questions answered

- **Who forwards a letter to the AI mailbox, and where?** In R1, a person of the firm (usually formalities staff) forwards the office letter from Outlook to the firm's own AI mailbox address (firm settings; for example akten@ the firm's domain). The mailbox accepts only mail that Exchange authenticated as internal. The test firm has no real AI mailbox: `acceptance/seed-letters.mjs` sent the 18 letters through a fake mailbox `ai-mailbox@seed-firm.test`, each from a formalities person of the seed firm (Sabine, Tobias or Miriam) who may see the matter. Those persons are not on the matter team, so the screen says "a person outside the team". Lena cannot forward a letter on the test firm today. To check: letters from the MyEPO Mailbox may have a text layer, unlike the register copies in the seed firm.


## Summary (end of QA, 2026-11-06)

50 findings. 15 block a pilot under the stop rule. The rest are tickets, seed-data fixes or questions.

**Works:** the matter walls (list, page and direct address), the #324 fix (Jonas sees no letter and cannot open one by address), the deadline calculation with its steps and rules, and the agent's honest answer when it has no data.

**Blocking, by group:**
- Identity and access: no sign-out (1), cannot switch account on one PC (39), every person is "Unnamed person" (25).
- The person layer: the first screen does not know the Matters (2), the list hides an unconfirmed deadline (20), no one is named as the next person to act and there is no way to ask (22, 31).
- Getting a letter to its matter: no page image (9), filing offered but refused for scans (10, #325), no screen to enter an office event by hand (44).
- Finding things: no search (15), the deadline row does not open (23), the date column can be clipped (16, to measure).
- Copy: the empty Intake says letters file themselves (41).
- The agent: `record_read` answers `not_configured` on the firm, so the agent cannot read any matter (45).

**Main finding (38):** the product is a chain (letter, matter, office event, deadline, confirmation, agent work, report, reply). It breaks at the letter-to-matter step, and it has no person layer (names, my list, the next step). The matter dossier and "my list" must be two views of the same tasks, and the agent's chats belong on the matter.

**Local firm result (os PR 74, 2026-11-06):** the local firm reproduced finding 45 and found three more defects behind it, so `record_read` cannot pass on any firm today: (1) the firm patch row (`flowleap-record` versus `flowleap-record-http`); (2) the spawner does not join a new runtime to the Firm Record's network; (3) the record reads runtime tokens only at start, but the spawner writes a new runtime's token later; (4) the record sends `responsible: null` where the harness needs a string (`invalid_answer`).
